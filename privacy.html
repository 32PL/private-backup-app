Private Backup privacy policy

Private Backup is a private, single-user app run by its owner on the owner's own computer.

  • It uses Google Drive access only to store and read the owner's own encrypted backup files in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • It does not collect, sell or share any data with anyone.
  • It sends no data to any server other than Google Drive.
  • Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the owner, through the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
